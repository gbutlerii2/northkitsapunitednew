# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date: November 17, 2026**

North Kitsap United (or “NKU”) is the public name of a community project operated by the Rotary Club of Kingston-North Kitsap, a Washington nonprofit corporation (the “Club,” “we,” “us,” or “our”). North Kitsap United is not represented on this website as a separate legal entity. This Privacy Policy describes information handled through this website, how it may be used and shared, and the choices available to you.

## 1. Organizations and Roles

The Club operates the website and receives general website submissions. Gifts promoted through the website are made to the Kingston-North Kitsap Rotary Foundation (the “Foundation”), a separate Washington nonprofit corporation recognized as tax-exempt under IRC § 501(c)(3) (EIN 20-2960627), and are designated for North Kitsap United, subject to the Foundation’s gift-acceptance authority and applicable law.

The YMCA of Pierce and Kitsap Counties is identified as an NKU project partner. Project partners, property owners, prospective property owners, sellers, public agencies, contractors, volunteers, fundraising platforms, payment processors, website hosts, analytics providers, and other service providers are separate persons or organizations. Their participation does not by itself create a partnership, joint venture, agency, fiduciary relationship, employment relationship, or authority to bind another participant.

## 2. Information We Collect

We may collect information you voluntarily provide, including:

- name, email address, telephone number, mailing address, and communication preferences;

- questions, messages, inquiries, update subscriptions, and unsubscribe requests;

- gift or pledge information, including amount, timing, intended method, recognition name, anonymity preference, tribute information, and optional comments;

- information relevant to proposed gifts of securities, donor-advised fund grants, individual retirement account distributions, real estate, or other property. Please do not submit account numbers, credentials, tax identification numbers, payment card data, or other highly sensitive information through our forms;

- information about in-kind offers, including business and contact information, licenses or registrations, proposed goods or services, availability, and dates;

- volunteer interests, skills, availability, age confirmation when relevant, emergency or screening information collected through a separate authorized process, and any accommodations requested; and

- any other information you voluntarily choose to send in form submissions.

We may also collect limited technical or usage information, such as pages visited, approximate traffic patterns, referring pages, device and browser information, IP address or approximate location, cookie or similar-technology identifiers, security logs, and performance information, depending on the tools actually enabled.

## 3. Donation and Payment Information

For card and bank-transfer gifts, a donor may provide contact, gift, and recognition information through our site and then continue to a Foundation donation page hosted by Zeffy. Zeffy and its payment processor, Stripe, collect and process payment credentials under their own terms and privacy practices. We do not intend to receive or store full card or bank-account numbers on this website. We may receive transaction, donor, contact, gift, date, status, recognition, and related information needed to record, acknowledge, reconcile, or administer a gift.

If a payment credential is accidentally submitted to us outside the designated payment pages, we may delete or securely handle it as circumstances require, but no transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## 4. How We Collect and Use Information

We may collect information through website forms, email, our donation service, vendors, analytics or security tools, embedded content, cookies or similar website technologies (such as cookies - if enabled), and direct communications. We may use information to:

- respond to inquiries and provide requested information;

- process, record, reconcile, acknowledge, and administer gifts and pledges;

- contact a person about completing a proposed noncash or other gift;

- apply donor-recognition and anonymity preferences, subject to legal recordkeeping and disclosure requirements;

- evaluate in-kind and volunteer offers, including qualifications, licensing, insurance, screening, scheduling, safety, conflicts, and project needs;

- maintain update lists and communication preferences;

- operate, secure, troubleshoot, audit, and improve the website, forms, database, communications, and fundraising administration;

- prevent fraud, misuse, security incidents, or unlawful activity;

- comply with law, tax, accounting, grant, insurance, litigation-hold, and recordkeeping obligations; and

- protect the rights, safety, property, and operations of the Club, Foundation, project participants, donors, volunteers, users, and others.

## 5. How We Share Information

We do not sell personal information for money. We may disclose information as/if reasonably necessary:

- to the Foundation for gift acceptance, receipting, accounting, reconciliation, donor service, legal compliance, and administration;

- to vendors that operate or support the website, forms, mailbox, database, communications, analytics, security, donation platform, payment processing, professional services, or records;

- to project partners, including the YMCA Partner, when needed to evaluate or coordinate an in-kind or volunteer offer, and only to the extent reasonably appropriate;

- to accountants, attorneys, insurers, auditors, banks, investment custodians, contractors, or governmental authorities when and where appropriate;

- in connection with suspected fraud, misuse, threats, claims, disputes, legal process, or a security incident;

- as directed or consented to by the person who supplied the information; or

- as otherwise permitted or required by law.

A donor’s public recognition name may be displayed only when the donor has opted in or otherwise authorized recognition. Gift amounts are not intended to be publicly displayed. Legal, tax, accounting, audit, or regulatory records may still identify a donor notwithstanding a public anonymity preference.

We use information collected thru the website to:

- Respond to questions, comments and other inquiries

- Record gifts and pledges, send receipts and acknowledgments – including thank you’s

- Contact you to help complete a gift you told us about

- Recognize donors who choose to be listed, by the name they choose and never with gift amounts

- Consider in-kind offers in the construction planning process

- Maintain and manage the updates list

- Process unsubscribe requests and communication preferences

- Improve website content, usability and performance

- Monitor basic website traffic and technical issues

- Protect the website and help prevent misuse

- Support community communication related to the North Kitsap United project

We keep information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, including to:

- Respond to inquiries

- Maintain mailing lists and communication preferences

- Keep basic administrative records

- Keep gift and pledge receipt records for as long as the Foundation needs them for its financial and tax records

- Address technical, legal or security concerns

We may periodically review and delete information that is no longer needed.

## 6. Third-Party Services

Some information submitted thru this website may be processed or stored using third-party service providers. For example, website forms send your information by email to <nku@rotaryknk.org> and gift and contact records are kept in a private campaign database used by the NKU team. Credit/Debit card and bank transfer gifts are processed by Zeffy and its payment processor, Stripe. Embedded videos may be provided through YouTube. Website analytics may also be provided by other third-party analytic tools and resources. Those third parties may process information according to their own terms, privacy policies, and technical practices.

## 7. Sensitive Information and Safer Channels

This website is intended for general community information and engagement. Please do not submit highly sensitive information such as Social Security numbers, driver’s license or passport numbers, medical records, insurance records, passwords, full financial-account numbers, payment-card data, private keys, or other similarly sensitive records through general website forms or ordinary email. If you need to contact us about a matter that may involve sensitive information, please use caution and contact us via <nku@rotaryknk.org> first so an appropriate method and next steps can be considered. Email and web forms are not guaranteed to be encrypted end to end or free from interception.

## 8. Cookies, Analytics, and Embedded Services

The site may use cookies, analytics, security, hosting, anti-spam, embedded-video, mapping, font, or similar technologies. The providers actually used at launch should be identified in the final posted policy or an adjoining cookie notice. A user may be able to manage cookies through browser settings, but disabling them may affect site features. We do not currently state that we respond to browser “do not track” signals unless counsel and the web developer confirm that capability.

## 9. Retention and Security

We use administrative, technical, and physical safeguards that we consider reasonable for the nature of the information and the resources of a volunteer-led community project. No safeguard, website, email system, database, or transmission method is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

We retain information only as reasonably necessary for the purposes described here, including donor and gift records maintained by the Foundation, legal and tax requirements, communications preferences, operational records, disputes, security, insurance, and authorized archival purposes. Retention periods may vary by record type. We may aggregate, deidentify, correct, archive, or delete information when appropriate, subject to legal holds and recordkeeping duties.

If we discover a security incident, we will evaluate and respond based on the facts, applicable contracts, insurance requirements, and law. This statement does not promise notice where notice is not legally required.

## 10. Your Choices and Requests

You may ask to unsubscribe from updates, change donor-recognition preferences prospectively and/or anonymously, or request that we review or correct contact information you submitted. Requests may be sent to <nku@rotaryknk.org>. We may need to verify identity and authority before acting. We may deny, limit, or retain information where permitted or required for tax, gift, legal, security, fraud-prevention, accounting, archival, or other legitimate purposes. Changes may not remove information already lawfully published, distributed, backed up, or retained by an independent third party.

## 11. Children’s Privacy

The website is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 18. We do not knowingly seek personal information from children under 18 through general website forms. Parents or legal guardians who believe a child submitted personal information should contact us. Volunteer participation by minors, if permitted, may require separate parent or guardian consent, supervision, screening, and program rules.

## 12. External Sites and Cross-Border Processing

External links, downloads, embedded content, vendors, and partner sites are governed by their own practices. Information may be processed in locations outside Washington. We are not responsible for a third party’s independent privacy, security, accessibility, availability, or content practices, although we may select and manage providers with reasonable care.

## 13. Changes and Contact

We may update this policy as the project, website, vendors, forms, law, or data practices change. The posted Effective Date controls. Material changes may be highlighted when practical, but prior notice of changes to this policy is not promised unless required by law.
